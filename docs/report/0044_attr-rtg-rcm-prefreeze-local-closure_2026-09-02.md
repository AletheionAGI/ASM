2. ordered actual principals, threshold M, trust roots, public keys e signed commitments;
3. attested HSM, private all-N reveal, key lifecycle e first/only invocation counter;
4. predeclared future beacon, external ledger inclusion e independent TSA;
5. independently administered watchdog/relay e real 24 h wire/reconnect/dead-host captures;
6. external WORM retention, trusted clock e signing-key custody/rotation/revocation;
7. remote measured boot e continuous T0–T5 read-set/syscall/device/ioctl/no-egress enforcement;
8. nova revisão exata após os sete grupos acima existirem.

Esses fatos não podem ser fabricados por código local. Test keys, local filesystem e preflight schemas não substituem administração independente.

Terminal checkpoints e artifacts de treino/calibration não são blockers do preregistration freeze; pertencem ao implementation seal posterior.
